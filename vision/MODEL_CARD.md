## Model

- **Task:** classify one road camera frame as `dry`, `wet`, `snow_partial`, `snow_covered`, `ice`, `unusable`.
- **Architecture:** MobileNetV3-Small (default) or EfficientNet-B0, fine-tuned from ImageNet weights with `vision/train.py` (OP-202).
- **Run:** `none yet`, seed n/a, PyTorch n/a, evaluated not yet.
- **Weights:** published as a GitHub release asset (`model.onnx` and `labels.json`), never committed.
- **Use in OpenPath:** the vision worker (OP-204) reads 511 Alberta cameras. An `ice` reading, or `wet` while the weather watch is active, becomes **ice risk** at medium confidence on crossings within 50 m. `unusable` becomes `unknown`, never `dry`, and a reading changes only after two agreeing frames.

## Known limits

- **Black ice is often invisible.** A thin clear layer can look like a dry or wet road. The model reports what the picture shows, and OpenPath combines `wet` with the weather watch for ice risk. It never claims to detect black ice.
- **Night.** Low light, noise and point lights hide surface texture. Frames that show too little should be `unusable`, but some night frames will be misread.
- **Glare and low sun.** Glare on a wet or icy road can look like snow or like a dry, washed-out surface.
- **Weather on the lens.** Water, snow or salt on the camera blurs the view; these frames should be `unusable`.
- **Coverage.** Trained and tested only on Alberta 511 cameras (and RSCD). Accuracy on other cameras, angles or regions is unknown.
- **Small test set.** Results come from a few cameras over a short period; one camera's quirks can move the numbers by several points.
